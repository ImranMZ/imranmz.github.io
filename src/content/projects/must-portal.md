Built a three-console portal for Multan University of Science and Technology, separating
student, admin, and faculty workflows into distinct interfaces. The rewrite focused on
replacing dated screens with a consistent layout system and shared component primitives
instead of copy-pasted pages.

Stack: React, Vite, TypeScript.
