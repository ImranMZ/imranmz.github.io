A job aggregator that scores postings against a profile and generates cover letters,
interview practice questions, and a skill-gap report. Adds a career-path view and a
gamified tracker so applications stay visible instead of disappearing into a spreadsheet.

Stack: JavaScript, AI-assisted matching and generation.
