About Me

Who I Am

I work at the intersection of public service and technology. As a Public Service Assistant in a Punjab Police IT environment, my day-to-day includes front desk support, data entry, report processing, system monitoring, database tasks, and everyday IT support. Accuracy matters — small mistakes create bigger problems downstream.

That experience showed me how much organizations depend on technology, and why understanding how systems work — and how they fail — matters as much as knowing how to use them. That’s why I’m pursuing a BS in Computer Science: not just to operate tools, but to understand, improve, and protect them.

Currently focused on: cybersecurity fundamentals, networking, databases, and automation to reduce repetitive manual work.
